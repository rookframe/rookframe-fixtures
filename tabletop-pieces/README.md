
## Tabletop Props 0.10.0

This optional data-only Package keeps the existing Crate and approved bookshelf
and adds the unchanged Poly Haven Wooden Crate 01 and Wine Barrel 01 formerly
shipped by the application. Those two models are CC0, authored by James Ray Cock
(https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_crate_01 and https://polyhaven.com/a/wine_barrel_01).
Their scale and collision geometry are unchanged; ordinary Godot scenes replace
the imported source GLTF payloads. Source files remain under source/starter-props.

Miniatures, Stone and Wood now belong to Rookframe's built-in collection.
This release ships no Miniatures, Wall Styles or Surface Finishes and has no
System-specific compatibility restriction. Install by its public Manifest:
https://github.com/rookframe/rookframe-fixtures/releases/download/tabletop-props-v0.10.0/Tabletop-Props-0.10.0.json
